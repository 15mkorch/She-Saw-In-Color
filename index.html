Enter the World
The World

The world has gone grey.

The Shedim

For centuries, the shedim — unseen supernatural forces — have been stealing "the color" from humanity. One by one, all humans have silently fallen, all except for one ordinary girl: Kailani.

Kailani

But Kailani's busy engineering a plan to escape the dangers of her family, hiding who she really is, and holding onto a dream she's been told her whole life to give up.

The Camu

She isn't fighting alone. The camu — guardians working just behind the veil — are waging an invisible war to protect her, and the last flicker of color left in the world.

The Stakes

Because if Kailani goes grey, all that makes the world worth saving will be lost forever.

For fans of Kathleen Glasgow · Tracy Deonn · Dual POV · Contemporary Fantasy

Meet the Characters

Joseph

The Ancient Guardian

Joseph is an ancient guardian assigned to protect Kailani from birth. He didn't expect to be carrying the burden of a mission so big that everything he knows depends on it. Not only must he confront the darkness closing in on Kailani, but as he learns more about his enemy, he must wrestle with his past to save the future.

Kailani

The Last Seer

Kailani's a seventeen-year-old star student from Green Grove, Indiana. She has spent her whole life hiding in the background, quietly architecting a meticulous plan to escape the life she was born into. Beneath the surface, she carries more secrets than anyone around her knows. What she doesn't know is the biggest of all.

Artibak

Agent of the Grey

Artibak moves like shadow: patient, precise, and impossible to predict. He's highly ranked among the shedim and feared for his unmatched ability to pull humanity into the grey. His tactics perplex both the camu who fight against him and the shedim who fight beside him, leaving everyone with the same uneasy question: what is he really after?

Will she step into the light? Or let the grey win?
About the Author

Mary Korch

Mary Korch writes stories that live at the intersection of the seen and unseen — where ordinary life brushes up against the magical, the mysterious, and the beautiful. As a former digital nomad who spent years working and living across dozens of countries, she brings a rare cross-cultural lens to the craft of storytelling.

Mary believes the world is far more vivid than most of us dare to notice. She Saw in Color is her first novel — written for the ones still willing to look.
